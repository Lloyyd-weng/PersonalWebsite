This site used to be a collection of case studies from a few years back. They
served their time, but I've moved on — so instead of patching the old pages, I
tore everything down and rebuilt from zero.

The new site is intentionally small: a home page, a place for [work](/work),
and this blog. It's built with Astro, styled with Tailwind, and every page is
plain static HTML — nothing to load, nothing to wait for.

The old case studies aren't gone, just archived. And there's space reserved
here for a few experiments I've been meaning to try. More on that later.
